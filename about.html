About Us

Discover the story behind Charles Retail Therapy

Our History

Charles Retail Therapy was founded to address the gap between high-end fashion and affordability for students and young professionals. The business sources pre-loved designer and sportswear brands from individuals who wish to donate or sell their items at accessible prices.

Mission Statement

To empower young people to look and feel their best by making premium fashion accessible, affordable, and sustainable.

Vision Statement

To become the go-to online destination for affordable second-hand branded fashion in South Africa, promoting a circular economy.

Sustainability Commitment

We are committed to reducing textile waste and promoting sustainable fashion practices. Every purchase contributes to a more circular economy and helps reduce the environmental impact of the fashion industry.

Brand Narrative

Charles Retail Therapy is more than a store. It is part of a cultural movement that values creativity, individuality, and smart spending. We celebrate the thrift culture and empower our customers to express their personal style through high-quality pre-loved fashion.
